Ethical Issues in AI and Cybersecurity

While AI is very useful for penetration testing, it can also be used in ways that go against the goals of cybersecurity professionals.

AI-Powered Cyberattacks: One ethical issue that can arise is the use of AI to assist cyberattacks. Most AI systems have safeguards that prevent users from generating malware or obtaining information related to criminal activities. However, many people attempt to bypass these safeguards through techniques known as "jailbreaks." Once these restrictions are removed, AI can potentially be used to identify vulnerabilities or assist in creating malicious software. This creates additional challenges for penetration testers, who must work quickly to discover and patch vulnerabilities before attackers can exploit them.

Handling Sensitive Information: Penetration testers also face ethical concerns regarding information handling. Depending on the scope of a security assessment, testers may gain access to emails, passwords, payment information, or other confidential data. The stakes become even higher when working with large organizations that store highly sensitive information. Penetration testers must ensure that any information they discover remains confidential and is only shared with authorized personnel.
