Interesting Industries

An industry that has always interested me is cybersecurity. Ever since I was younger, I have been interested in how security works in things like games, websites, and computers. This interest played a role in many things I liked to do, but it was very prominent in games I played such as Minecraft or Roblox. When it came to video games, I always tried my best to find glitches, exploits, bugs, and vulnerabilities. At that age, I always saw it as a way to gain an advantage over others or a way to flaunt my technical knowledge, but now I realize I can turn it into an actual career.

Career

Since I knew how to small vulnerabilites in games and all, I wanted to find out if there were any jobs in cybersecurity that utilized my skill, which led me to find out about Penetration Testing. Penetration Testing (also shorted to Pen Testing), is defined as "A method of testing where testers target individual binary components or the application as a whole to determine whether intra or intercomponent vulnerabilities can be exploited to compromise the application, its data, or its environment resources." [https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/penetration_testing]
